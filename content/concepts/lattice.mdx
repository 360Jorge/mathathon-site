## Definition

A lattice $L$ is the set of integer combinations $\mathbb{Z}b_1+\dots+\mathbb{Z}b_n$ of linearly independent vectors, together with a bilinear form recorded by the Gram matrix $G_{ij}=b_i\cdot b_j$.

## Example

The lattice $A_2$ has Gram matrix $\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$ and discriminant $3$.
